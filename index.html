EtSi Publisher

EtSi Publisher is a small personal tool that uploads short "What if…" fiction videos, created by their owner, to the owner's own TikTok accounts (French, English and Spanish editions). Videos are clearly labelled as AI-generated fiction.

How it works

  1. Click Connect TikTok account and authorise EtSi Publisher with TikTok.
  2. EtSi Publisher (on the owner's computer) receives the authorisation and checks the connected creator.
  3. Each new "What if…" video is uploaded with its caption and the AI-generated content label.
