Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

EtSi Publisher stores only the access tokens TikTok provides after you connect your account. They are kept on the owner's computer, used solely to upload videos and read basic account information (username, video statistics), and never shared or sold.

No data from other users is collected. No cookies or analytics are used on this website.

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings; the tokens then stop working. To ask for deletion, open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
